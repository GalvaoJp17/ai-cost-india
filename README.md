## The group buy trap

Telegram and WhatsApp resellers advertise ChatGPT Plus at ₹399 to ₹448 per month via UPI. The real price you pay is risk:

1. Stolen account inventory is openly traded on Telegram black markets with fake money back guarantees
2. Fake payment screenshot scams are mainstream (PhonePay publishes its own anti fraud guide for them)
3. A September 2026 phishing wave impersonated ChatGPT subscription billing alerts to steal OpenAI credentials
4. Shared accounts get banned without warning, and your chat history goes with them

Complaints can be filed at consumercomplaints.in, India's consumer forum.

## The free alternative

JustBot (https://justbot.co) gives you autonomous AI bots with their own computer, persistent memory, and 1,500+ integrations. Free tier, no card. Paid starts at $10 per month, roughly ₹850, one third of Grok Bot, 10x lower cost per token.

Hinglish summary: Grok Bot ₹2,600 per month. JustBot ₹850. Teen guna sasta, 10x zyada tokens. Free me start, card nahi maangte.
